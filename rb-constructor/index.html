OUR METHODOLOGY

From Blueprint to Key Handover in 4 Steps

A streamlined, transparent timeline so you always know what happens next on your construction site.

01

Site Consultation

We perform an on-site soil and level survey, listen to your requirements, and map out initial feasibility and budget parameters.

02

Plan & Itemized Quote

Receive detailed 2D/3D architectural drawings, structural calculations, and a transparent itemized cost breakdown with timeline.

03

Execution & Quality Checks

Our dedicated engineers execute construction on-site with multi-tier quality checks, cube testing, and weekly client updates.

04

Inspection & Key Handover

Final deep-cleaning, joint structural audit, utility testing, and formal handover of keys with written warranty documentation.
